# The jersey faces

Eighteen athletic lettering faces, each subset to the digits, the capitals, the space, the apostrophe, the hyphen and the period and converted to WOFF2 (about 58 KB in all). Every one is distributed on dafont.com as **free for personal use**; the site is non-commercial. The family name in jersey.css is J-<id>.
